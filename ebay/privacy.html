2. Connecting to eBay (authorization)

The application only accesses your eBay account if you explicitly allow it. Sign-in is handled by eBay's own authorization flow (OAuth 2.0). The application never collects or stores your eBay password. Instead, eBay issues the application an access token that allows it to use the granted functions on your behalf.

You can revoke access at any time:
My eBay → Account → Account settings → Third-party app access → remove the application.
After revocation the token becomes invalid and the application can no longer access your account.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the application's functions on your behalf – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract or contract-like usage relationship).
  • Consent to access your eBay account via eBay's authorization – Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (see section 2).
  • Security, error analysis and abuse prevention – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

5. What we do NOT do with the data

  • Data is not sold and not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.
  • Data is not used for profiling, scoring or any purpose other than the functions described.
  • eBay buyer data is not used outside of order fulfilment and not stored for our own marketing purposes.

Processing complies with the terms of the eBay Developer Program (eBay API License Agreement).

6. Retention and deletion

  • Access tokens are stored only as long as the connection to your eBay account exists. They are deleted when you revoke access or disconnect.
  • Listing, order and buyer data is stored only as long as required for the respective function and is deleted afterwards, unless statutory retention obligations apply.
  • Technical logs are deleted after 30 days at the latest.

eBay account deletion: The application subscribes to eBay's Marketplace Account Deletion/Closure Notifications. If you close your eBay account or request deletion of your data from eBay, eBay notifies us and we promptly delete all data stored in relation to your eBay account.

You can also request deletion of your data at any time by e-mail to hn_player@hotmail.de.
